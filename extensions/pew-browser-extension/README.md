# ⚡ Pew Browser Extension (Video & Media Grabber)

> IDM-like automatic video detection and media download grabber for Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and Chromium browsers.

---

## 🌟 Features

- 🎬 **Automatic Media Detection:** Sniffs and catches video/audio requests (`.mp4`, `.mkv`, `.webm`, `.m3u8` HLS, `.mpd` DASH).
- ⚡ **Floating IDM-style Download Widget:** Directly hovers on video players with a draggable `⚡ Download Video` pill.
- 📋 **Format & Quality Breakdown:** Inspects streams, Content-Length, and filename on the fly.
- 🚀 **1-Click Forwarding to Pew:** Passes the download URL and cookies straight into the Pew Download Manager desktop engine.

---

## 🛠️ How to Install in Your Browser

1. Open your browser and go to the extensions page:
   - **Google Chrome:** `chrome://extensions`
   - **Microsoft Edge:** `edge://extensions`
   - **Brave:** `brave://extensions`
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
3. Click the **"Load unpacked"** (বা "প্যাক না করা লোড করুন") button.
4. Select the directory:
   `C:\Users\RAFIQ\Projects\Pew-download-manager\extensions\pew-browser-extension`
5. Done! The **Pew Grabber** icon will appear in your browser toolbar. Now play any video on YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, or any website to see the floating download button!
